How to film

  • Hold the phone upright so the whole tube and the green and red lines are in the frame.
  • Don't hold the phone in your hand. Use a stand or lean it against something. Any shaking makes the speed wrong.
  • Choose Slo-mo in the Camera app. Start just before you drop the ball, and stop when it reaches the bottom.

Ball position

If the dots fall on a straight line, the ball fell at a constant speed. This speed, where the ball neither speeds up nor slows down in the liquid, is called the terminal velocity.

Speed vs. depth

The gray band is the region between the two lines. If the curve is flat there, the ball was already at terminal velocity when you measured it.

Reynolds number and drag coefficient

The Reynolds number (Re) tells you how much the liquid's viscosity (how thick and sticky it is) controls the flow around the ball. The smaller Re is, the more viscosity dominates.

The drag coefficient (CD) tells you how much resistance the ball feels. At terminal velocity, the ball's weight minus the buoyant force equals the drag, so you can work backward from the measured speed to CD.
