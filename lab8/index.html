Lab 8: Drag & Lift CE 319F

Drag & Lift

Record a sphere falling through fluid. The app tracks it automatically and calculates terminal velocity, Re, and CD.

How It Works

  1. Record a slo-mo video of the ball dropping through the tube
  2. Calibrate by tapping the two marker lines on the tube
  3. The app auto-tracks the ball frame by frame
  4. Get velocity profile, terminal velocity, Re, and CD

Theory

At terminal velocity, drag force = submerged weight:

FD = ½ CD ρ V2 A = (ρs − ρf) · (π/6) D³ · g

For smooth spheres with Re < 1000:

CD = 24/Re + 3/√Re + 0.34
